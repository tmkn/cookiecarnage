# Story

## Backstory

You are Chad De'Alloc, a retired "Garbage Collector" who has been called back into action to clean up a rogue AI.

You see, "Garbage Collecting" was a vital part of keeping websites fast and responsive. It was a thankless and dangerous job, but someone had to do it. That is, until AI could do it for us. But now, the AI has gone rogue and is causing all sorts of havoc on the internet.

With many of the original "Garbage Collectors" gone, you are humanity's only hope of saving the internet and its cat videos.

## Trace

Trace is the #1 JavaScript framework.

## LeCrev

LeCrev is a well-known hosting company in the JavaScript world. It has a popular Trace framework called LeCrev.js.

However, LeCrev intentionally tries to blur the line between itself and Trace, piggybacking on Trace's success and luring unsuspecting developers into its ecosystem. Once developers adopt LeCrev.js, they are locked into that ecosystem and cannot easily switch to another framework—let alone host their website elsewhere—because LeCrev.js is tightly coupled to its hosting platform. This lucrative business model has ensured that LeCrev maintains a strong foothold in the JavaScript world.

Trace is a client-side framework, but LeCrev has made it a server-side framework to further increase its foothold and lock-in. Unfortunately, this has made it easier for the rogue AI to take control of the internet, as it can now manipulate the DOM on the server side.
